Privacy policy

Effective October 2, 2026. This policy covers RTS Listings, an internal tool RTS One LLC uses to list and manage its own items for sale on eBay.

Who uses it

Only RTS One LLC. The tool is not offered to the public and no outside eBay seller connects to it.

What it accesses

With the seller's permission through eBay's sign-in, the tool reads and changes that seller's own listings, inventory, business policies and orders, and reads public eBay listing data for pricing.

What it keeps

It keeps records of our own items: specifications, test results, photos, prices and the eBay listing number. It does not store buyer names, addresses, emails, phone numbers or payment details. Order information is read from eBay only when we ship and is not saved by the tool.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone. Data stays on systems RTS One LLC controls.

Deletion

Because the tool holds no buyer data, there is nothing to delete when an eBay member closes their account. For any question, or to ask what we hold, email will@rtsone.com.

RTS One LLC, Valparaiso, Indiana
